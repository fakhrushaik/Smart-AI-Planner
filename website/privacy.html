PRIVACY & YOUR DATA

Your plans belong to you.

This static preview keeps planning data in your browser. We do not operate an account service, receive your schedule, or send your entries to an AI provider.

Effective October 16, 2026 · Applies to this GitHub Pages preview.

What the planner stores

Events contain a title, date, time, category, and optional location. Tasks contain a title, date, category, and completion state. By default these entries are held in the current browser tab and disappear when the page is reloaded or closed.

If you enable “Save my planner on this device,” these entries are stored in your browser’s local storage until you turn saving off, delete the data, or clear website data. Your solid-mode display preference is also saved locally when you change it. No application cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, remote fonts, or third-party scripts are used.

Your controls

  • Access and correct: view and edit entries in the planner.
  • Export: download tasks and events as JSON, or events as an iCalendar file, in Preferences.
  • Import: restore a previously exported JSON file in Preferences. Imported files are read in your browser only, checked before use, and only the title, date, time, category, location, and completion fields are kept. They are not uploaded to us or to any AI provider.
  • Delete: “Delete planner data” removes saved tasks and events from this browser. Turn off device saving to remove the saved copy while keeping the current tab’s working data.
  • Display settings: switch solid mode off to save that choice, or clear this website’s storage to remove the display preference record.

Copies exported to files, imported into another calendar, or kept on other devices must be deleted separately. Other open planner tabs retain their working copies and can save them again; close those tabs after deletion. JSON export is a readable backup; this preview does not yet restore JSON files.

Hosting and external links

GitHub hosts this website. GitHub documents that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Its collection, retention, and handling of hosting data are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. See also GitHub Pages hosting information.

External links are not loaded until you follow them. If you submit a GitHub issue, GitHub processes that submission and public issues are visible to others. Do not include private schedules or other sensitive information.

Shared devices and sensitive information

Browser storage is not an encrypted vault. GitHub project sites on the same hostname share a browser origin; other scripts hosted on that same origin can access local storage. Anyone who can use your browser profile may be able to view saved plans. Avoid entering passwords, health records, financial information, student records, or other confidential data. Device saving is optional and off until you choose it.

Privacy questions and requests

This is a Team 59 student project. For a general privacy question, open an issue without personal information. We cannot retrieve, correct, or erase your local planner data remotely. Use the controls above for local data and GitHub’s privacy channels for hosting data.

Applicable rights depend on your jurisdiction and on the party processing the information. This notice describes the implementation; it is not a claim of universal GDPR, CCPA, FERPA, or other legal compliance. A production service with accounts or integrations will require a new assessment and an updated notice before those features are enabled.
