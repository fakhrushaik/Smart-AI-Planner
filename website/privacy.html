PRIVACY & YOUR DATA

Your plans belong to you.

Choose a local demo or sign in for cloud storage. This notice explains both.

Updated October 24, 2026 · Team 59 student preview.

Local demo

At the local demo, plans stay in memory unless you enable device saving. Optional local storage holds event titles, dates, times, categories, locations, and task completion. Reloading or closing a tab discards unsaved local plans. The display preference is saved separately.

Signed-in accounts

Supabase processes your email address, authentication credentials, session information, and any optional display name. Passwords go directly over HTTPS to Supabase Auth; the planner does not store readable passwords in its tables or browser storage. Supabase also stores your signed-in events and tasks, creation/update times, and a revision used to detect conflicting edits. The project is hosted in US East (North Virginia).

Ownership policies restrict each account to its own profile and plans. Project administrators retain administrative access. Authentication and hosting providers may retain security logs, including connection metadata; their retention follows their service policies and project settings.

Sessions

The app stores authentication tokens in this tab’s session storage, not permanent local storage. It refreshes the short-lived access token while you use the app. Closing the tab normally removes its local session, but browser restore features can preserve tab state. Sign out on shared devices. Signing out revokes refresh sessions; already-issued access tokens can remain valid until their configured expiry. Account deletion removes account data and revokes sessions.

Imports, exports, and migration

JSON imports are validated and stripped to planner fields. In local demo mode they remain local. In signed-in mode, you confirm before the file replaces your cloud plans. If older local plans are found, the app offers to import them into the signed-in account; nothing is uploaded automatically, and the local copy is removed only after a confirmed save. Only import data that belongs to you.

JSON exports contain tasks and events; calendar exports contain events. Downloads remain separate files under your control. The example week is fictional and labeled as an example.

Access, correction, and deletion

  • View and edit your plans in Today, Calendar, and Tasks.
  • Export your plans in Preferences before leaving or deleting an account.
  • “Delete planner data” removes plans from the active mode: local storage in the local demo, or your signed-in cloud account.
  • “Delete account” in signed-in Preferences deletes your profile, plans, and Supabase sign-in account after confirmation.
  • Other tabs may retain working copies. Close them after deletion. Exported files, calendar copies, and provider logs/backups are not erased by the planner.
  • Clear this website’s browser data to remove local plans and saved display preferences. Turning solid mode off saves that choice rather than removing its record.

Cloud plans are retained until you delete them or the project is retired. Provider security logs and any backups may outlast deletion under their retention policies; we do not promise immediate erasure from every provider system.

Providers and external connections

GitHub Pages serves the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Login and signed-in planner pages connect only to this project’s Supabase HTTPS origin. The Supabase SDK is served from the site itself. No analytics, advertising, remote fonts, or tracking scripts are included. Apple and Google OAuth are implemented but unavailable until developer setup is complete; when enabled, choosing them sends you to the selected identity provider.

Read Supabase’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Shared devices and sensitive information

This is a student preview. Use fictional plans for the sprint review. Do not enter passwords in planner fields, health records, financial data, confidential student records, or data you are not permitted to use. Browser storage is not an encrypted vault. GitHub project sites on the same hostname share an origin, so other scripts on that origin can access browser storage. A dedicated origin and privacy review are needed before sensitive production use.

Questions and rights

Submit a general project question without private information. Public issues are visible to others. Use in-app controls for your plans and provider privacy channels for provider-held information. Rights depend on your jurisdiction and the relevant data controller. We do not claim universal GDPR, CCPA, FERPA, WCAG, or Apple compliance or certification.
