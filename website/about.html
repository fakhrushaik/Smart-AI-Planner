OUR STORY

Made for the days that don’t go to plan.

Classes overlap. Shifts change. A to-do list gets longer. We’re building a planner that makes those days a little easier to see.

A student project, shaped by real schedules

Smart AI Planner is a University of Cincinnati senior design project by Team 59. This website brings the supplied Figma concepts to life with a calm cream background, purple accents, and clear pastel categories.

What works today

Create and edit events, see a weekly calendar, manage tasks, save locally by choice, export your data, and adjust the display. The local demo needs no account; signed-in mode uses Supabase.

What comes next

Accounts and cloud planner storage are implemented for review. Calendar connections, reminders, AI suggestions, and provider sign-in setup remain future work. Any future data processing will need explicit controls and updated privacy information.

A little progress is still progress

We don’t measure your worth by a full calendar. This space is here to help you decide what matters, one day at a time.
