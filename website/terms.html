A few things to know.

Smart Planner is a free student-project preview for personal planning.

Effective October 16, 2026.

Use and availability

You may use this preview to organize your own tasks and events. Features may change or become unavailable. Keep separate copies of important plans and verify deadlines, appointments, and exported calendar times.

No account or professional advice

This version has no sign-in, live calendar sync, notifications, or AI service. It is not intended for medical, legal, financial, emergency, or other safety-critical scheduling.

You control what you enter. Do not include information you are not permitted to use. See the privacy notice for local storage, deletion, exports, and GitHub hosting.
