## Project Summary

The Smart Planner app is an AI-powered solution designed to help students overcome poor planning and time-management challenges. By integrating academic calendars, job schedules, and extracurricular activities into one platform, the app provides dynamic scheduling and smart prioritization.

## Problem Statement

College students juggle assignments, classes, jobs, and extracurricular activities. Without structured planning, students may experience academic difficulties and mental-health challenges. The contract cites research connecting poor task planning, insufficient progress monitoring, emotional depression, high neuroticism, low conscientiousness, and poorer academic performance with procrastination and psychological distress in higher education (Faure-Carvallo et al., 2025).

Traditional planners and calendars require manual input and may fail to adapt to personal habits. The proposed project addresses the need for more intelligent and adaptive planning tools.

## Proposed Solution

The Smart Planner app will respond to students' daily needs and habits through:

- **Academic calendar synchronization:** Automatically import class schedules, deadlines, and exams to reduce manual input.
- **Work and extracurricular integration:** Combine academic and non-academic responsibilities.
- **Dynamic scheduling and rescheduling:** Adjust plans in real time and propose new schedules when tasks or events are missed.
- **Smart task prioritization:** Rank tasks by urgency and importance to minimize procrastination.
- **Reminders and alerts:** Notify students of upcoming tasks and deadlines.
- **Progress monitoring and insights:** Track productivity and provide feedback to improve habits.

## Project Source

The idea originated from the team's shared experiences balancing academic, work, and personal obligations. The team recognized limitations in traditional planners and demand for AI-driven organization tools among students.

Requirements analysis included:

- Reviewing academic studies on student time-management challenges.
- Interviewing peers to identify common scheduling problems.
- Studying existing planning apps to evaluate gaps in current solutions.

The team formed during the Senior Design course selection process around a shared interest in developing a productivity tool.

## Project Objectives and Goals

Provide a responsive, intelligent planning system that helps students:

- Lower stress.
- Reduce procrastination.
- Enhance academic performance.
- Improve organization and productivity habits.

## Project Scope

Develop a functional application that helps student users improve time management through AI-powered scheduling. Work will be structured into milestones across two semesters of Senior Design.

### In scope

- User registration and secure login.
- Bidirectional academic and work calendar synchronization.
- AI task prioritization.
- Intelligent reminders based on prioritization.
- Dynamic event creation and schedule adjustments.
- Dashboard with productivity insights.

### Out of scope for the capstone timeline

- Full mobile app deployment to app stores.
- Advanced machine-learning recommendation engine.
- External API integrations beyond school and work calendars.
- Voice assistant features.

## Ethical Considerations

The contract emphasizes user-data privacy, transparency, and responsible AI use because the app handles sensitive schedule data, personal habits, and academic workloads. It cites Faure-Carvallo et al. (2025) in discussing secure data practices and minimizing psychological pressure from productivity systems.

The team's stated considerations include:

- Secure storage of user information.
- Avoiding manipulative or overly intrusive AI nudges.
- Transparency in AI scheduling decisions.
- FERPA-related considerations for academic data.
